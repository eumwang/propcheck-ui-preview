Method and reporting periods

Reference score before history deduction = payout/account 50% + firsthand reviews 20% + operational foundation 30%. This is not a payout-success or safety probability.

Firsthand-review score: 730 days

We use accepted original-text reviews with a discernible evaluation of the firm. Official/operator posts, promotion alone, personal trading results without a firm evaluation, wrong companies/products, unresolved holds and duplicates are separated or excluded. Sentiment percentages are not the score. Posts are grouped by author and month: positive 1, negative 0 and mixed 0.5, with a 180-day weight half-life and small-sample adjustment.

Author-level weighted averages and effective sample size n produce X = (weighted mean score × n + 43.03 × 6.11) / (n + 6.11). Author-level weighting limits the influence of prolific posters.

Review breakdown: selectable display period

365 days, 730 days and all collected dates update counts, shares and original-post lists together. Click positive, mixed or negative to see matching posts. Changing this display period does not recalculate overall scores or review tags.

Firsthand reports and payout score: all accepted dates

Displayed payout/issue shares differ from the adjusted payout score. Reports are grouped by author-month: receipt only 1, receipt mixed with other events 0.5 and no receipt 0. A mixed value does not establish resolution. P = (100 × sum of author-month mean outcomes + 86.9021 × 13.5824) / (author-month count + 13.5824). This component does not decay with time. Sparse evidence approaches the prior value; a high score alone does not imply reassurance.

Scope and limitations

Original-text review checks eligibility and what a post says. It does not independently authenticate trades, certificates, claims or firm responsibility. Holds are excluded from scoring and display. Duplicate identities and repeated events across months may remain. Complaint-category observations use a separate six-month dataset, not the 730-day scoring population.

Operational foundation evidence

Payout process 25, financial disclosure 25, operating structure 30 and support source scale 20. Verified issue penalty P is separate. Foundation contributes 30% to the overall score.

G uses the highest single eligible support source business or user-base scale metric, such as audited equity, revenue, defined real trading volume or dated verified financial-platform membership. Previous C scale credit is removed before G. Support source size is not a prop payout guarantee.

Additional rule-history deduction (approved v2)

Overall reference score = max(0, 0.50P + 0.20X + 0.30T − H). The P/X/T weights are unchanged.

Only confirmed incremental adverse changes applying to purchased/existing accounts, or confirmed unannounced S/A changes, qualify. S8/A4/B2/C1 points; take the maximum within one incident. Per firm: largest incident plus half the others, capped at 12. Warning rank alone does not trigger a deduction.

H = max(0, company history penalty R − verified weighted overall-score loss L from P for the same included incidents). A P report alone never erases the history penalty. Unresolved linkage or contribution holds new deductions; update P and its overlap credit in the same version.

Withdrawal alone, partial remedy or unverified remedy retains the penalty. Full withdrawal plus confirmed remedy for everyone affected halves it; a proven mistaken change is zero. No automatic expiry with age. These are editorial parameters, not safety probabilities.
