02

Know the costs and how orders are handled.

The products are crypto and TradFi perpetual futures. They work differently from FX/CFD accounts, and positions may incur funding fees while open. Check the trading hours for each instrument before using it.

No trading-fee markup. Funding fees are separate.

Trading fees follow Hyperliquid's standard maker / taker rates, with no markup from Hypernova. If you hold positions for longer, include separate funding fees in your profit-and-loss calculations.

Prices and order-book data reference Hyperliquid, but orders are processed by Hypernova's own engine. Because execution happens elsewhere, fills can differ from those on Hyperliquid itself.

04

Choose Tight, Low or Medium around your strategy and budget.

Tight | Lower entry fee, tighter loss allowance

At $400 for 100K, the upfront fee is lower. But you are aiming for $9,000 in profit with a $3,000 loss allowance: a target three times that allowance. It suits traders who can keep individual losses small.

Low | A fixed 6% overall loss allowance

If Tight's $3,000 would not cover your usual losing streaks, consider Low. A 100K account costs $999 and gives a $6,000 overall loss allowance. The target is 10% and daily loss limit 3%. The overall buffer is larger, but the daily allowance is the same as Tight's.

Medium | More room both daily and overall

If Low's 3% daily limit feels tight, Medium costs $1,350 for 100K—$351 more than Low. The target stays at 10%, while daily loss rises to 4% and overall loss to 7%. At the initial 100K balance, each allowance is $1,000 larger than Low's.

05

From closing a profit to receiving USDC.

You do not need to wait for a large profit before withdrawing. With no minimum payout or waiting period, you can collect a smaller gain too. A smart contract processes the request and pays in USDC, with the transfer record traceable on-chain. Arrival time depends on network conditions and other factors.

06

There are some restrictions on strategies and account numbers.

Watch the rules on external signals and strategy changes

Third-party copy trading and signals, cross-account hedging, and major strategy changes between evaluation and funding are restricted. Check the conditions for your own bots too, including use on a single account of your own.

Multiple accounts and loss checks

Evaluation and funded accounts have a combined cap of 300K, with at most 2 evaluations running at once. Daily limits use the UTC 00:00 balance, while monitoring is based on equity including unrealized gains and losses. Reaching a limit counts as a breach, so leave a buffer.

Who might it suit?

What I like about Hypernova is that you can wait for your best setups without then waiting for payout day. I would choose a plan by looking at my usual losing streak first. If the loss allowance is too small, freedom from trading-day requirements will not make the account easy to keep.
