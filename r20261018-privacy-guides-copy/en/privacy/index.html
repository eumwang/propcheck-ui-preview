Privacy policy

This is a provisional version with matters still under review. It distinguishes confirmed operations from adopted policies and unfinished procedures. Deletion procedures for individual storage locations and the handling conditions of external services still include matters under review.

1. Basic policy

PROP CHECK (the “Site”) will comply with laws concerning the protection of personal information and handle the information it obtains appropriately, within the scope necessary for its stated purposes.

2. Information collected and purposes of use

Contact, listing and report forms may collect the information entered, including names or contact details when provided, URLs, message content and report attachments. Submitted form content is processed and stored by Netlify, Inc. and may be handled outside Japan.

For inquiries, we collect the information you enter, such as your name, reply email address, inquiry and related URLs, to respond, check or correct published information, and address inquiries about rights.

For listing requests and case reports, we collect information such as the company concerned, report details, when the event occurred, the company’s response, publicly available URLs and contact details. We use this information to review, classify and edit possible listings, check facts and prevent fraudulent submissions.

We also use publicly available posts and similar information to analyze and classify information about companies and check the evidence for published content. Where public information includes personal information, we handle it only to the extent necessary.

3. Publication and management of information

We do not publish contact details or the received materials themselves. After review, submitted cases may be published as summaries with care taken not to identify individuals, or with publicly available supporting URLs.

We take necessary and appropriate security measures to prevent leakage, loss, damage and similar incidents. We review the need to retain information and endeavor to erase information without delay once it is no longer needed, except where legal retention obligations or similar requirements apply.

In addition to storage at Netlify, submissions to the case-report form are synchronized to and stored in a review ledger on the operational Windows PC. Cloud receipt and storage are separate from synchronization to the PC.

Our adopted retention and deletion policy covers form submissions, attachments, notification emails, case correspondence and their copies. The basic retention limit is 180 days from first receipt. If a case remains open, we record the reason for an extension and the next review date. We aim to delete the material within 30 days after the case closes. We aim to respond to deletion requests within 30 days of receipt. Ordinary recovery backups have a maximum of 30 days from creation; an earlier deadline for the original data takes priority. The policy calls for deleted information not to return after restoration. Public X research, rule ledgers and unrelated scoring evidence are outside this specific deletion scope.

A mechanism to prevent deleted information from being imported again was tested in an isolated environment and connected to the operational case collector. Automatic deletion at the deadline is not running. Registration and deletion of actual records, checks across every storage location, and prevention of reappearance after restoring whole backups are not complete.

Logs and similar records whose retention deadlines the Site cannot control through the service are treated separately from the Site’s retention policy.

Access controls still require confirmation. Please do not include passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information in a form.

4. External services and provision to third parties

When providing personal data to a third party, we obtain the individual’s prior consent, except where the law provides an exception. When outsourcing handling to the extent necessary to achieve the purposes of use, we appropriately supervise the service provider.

Form input is sent to and stored by the forms service of Netlify, Inc. It may be processed outside Japan. The case-report form currently collects submissions only. External AI classification, scoring and publication of form input remain stopped. Classification of public X posts by external AI is a separate process.

We use the TypeSafe AI API to classify public X posts. We may send the text of posts being classified, retrieved context from parent or quoted posts, expanded URLs, and information about the company or service concerned and attributes needed for classification. This is separate from external AI processing of form input.

According to TypeSafe’s public explanation, the service is hosted in the United States and inputs are not used for model training or fine-tuning. Retention is described as lasting for the period necessary to provide the service and related purposes; we do not guarantee a fixed retention period or immediate deletion.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The Site uses browser storage to retain display preferences. You can delete stored information through your browser settings.

At the settings check on October 18, 2026, Netlify Analytics is disabled. Separately, logs are handled for delivery monitoring and security. Log types and retention conditions vary with the service and settings used; this does not mean that these logs are deleted under the same 180-day and 30-day periods as the form retention policy.

Some English and Korean guide pages use Google’s image delivery service to display company logos. When these images load, the browser communicates with Google and its image delivery hosts under gstatic.com. Image-request URLs contain information about the company website whose logo is displayed.

These images are configured not to send the Site’s referrer information. On October 18, 2026, a fresh browser without a signed-in account was used to check the English and Korean guide indexes. User-Agent information was sent. Referer and Cookie headers and cookie-setting response headers were not observed. This result is limited to the environment tested; it does not guarantee the same behavior with every browser setting or existing cookie state.

Google’s published privacy policy describes the collection of IP addresses and other information through communications with its services. Suppressing referrer information on this Site does not make external-image communications anonymous. The provider’s policies apply to the handling of information at image delivery destinations.

You can restrict cookie acceptance and storage, or delete cookies, through your browser settings. Restricting cookies is not the same as stopping external images from loading. Some display elements or functions may become unavailable depending on the restrictions.
