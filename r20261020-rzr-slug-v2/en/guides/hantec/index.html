04

Most popular

Endurance 3STEP: our pick if you can give it time.

3STEP's appeal goes beyond price. Each stage targets 6%, with a fixed maximum loss allowance of 8%. There is no consistency rule, and the minimum 3 trading days do not all have to be winning days. You do not need extra trades simply to create profitable days.

After passing, there is still no consistency rule, and weekend holding and EAs are allowed. Being able to keep using your preferred method makes the three stages feel worthwhile. Funded accounts add a 3% floating-loss limit and restrictions around economic releases; read the payout and trading conditions below too.

Before buying bigger, keep a budget for another attempt.

The drawback is the road to passing. Even after you make 6%, the next account starts fresh. Do that three times, and a failure partway through also costs time.

100K for $299 makes a bigger account tempting, but I would avoid spending the entire budget on the first attempt. Decide how many attempts you can afford, then choose the account size within that budget.

05

Express 1STEP: another option for reaching that first payout.

Express is for getting through the evaluation quickly. With no minimum trading days, you can pass in as little as 1 day if you meet the conditions. At the extreme, even 1 trade could do it. Reach the 10% target on a good opportunity, and you need not keep trading just to fill out the calendar.

Express basicsDetails
Profit target10%
Daily / maximum loss5% / 6% (trails closed balance)
Evaluation periodNo minimum days or maximum time limit
Standard first payout14 days after the first funded trade / $20 minimum
Profit splitStandard 80%

After the first payout, the loss cushion gets smaller.

The first payout fixes the loss threshold at the initial balance. Withdraw all profits so the balance returns to the initial amount, and you have no loss cushion left. Keeping the account running requires leaving profit behind, so treating the first payout as a stopping point can make sense.

On a 100K account, if both the post-payout balance and loss threshold are $100,000, there is zero cushion for the next loss. That is a significant consideration if you want repeated payouts from the same account.

Ponchai's suggested approach

Take the payout, then fund the next plan.

I would withdraw the eligible profit rather than leave it in Express to keep trading. That money could buy Endurance or fund another attempt. I see Express more as a route to the first payout than an account to build over the long term.

The cash you receive is after the profit split. Factor in the next plan's purchase cost and the work of passing again.

Who it suits

For traders who want to reach a first payout without stacking multiple evaluation stages. Weekend holding and EAs are allowed, so it can suit a proven method within those rules.

A fast pass is not a same-day payout. First-payout on-demand access is a paid add-on. The standard 14-day wait, funded-account 3% floating-loss cap and restrictions around news still apply separately.

Instant: skip evaluation, then choose between three ways to trade.

Instant is an option if you want to skip the evaluation. But the three types work quite differently. Instant24 in particular ends after 24 hours, so you would choose another type for longer-term trading.

Instant Funding: start trading without an evaluation.

Instant Funding skips the evaluation and has no consistency rule. If your profits tend to cluster on particular days, it is worth checking before Lite or Instant24.

Watch the 3% floating-loss cap. The 6% daily and 6% total limits may look generous, but an open position can hit the floating-loss rule first. Standard payouts are every 14 days. Weekend holding and news-trading conditions vary with your chosen add-ons.

Source: Official Instant Funding display ↗

Instant Lite: a lower entry fee for steady profit-building.

Instant Lite starts without evaluation and keeps the entry fee down. However, profit must be spread across several days, making it awkward if you want a payout from a few large wins.

Each payout cycle needs 5 days earning at least 0.5% each. The best day must also be no more than 20% of total profit. The first 3% stays in the account as a non-withdrawable buffer. Limits include 3% daily loss, 5% maximum trailing loss and 1% floating loss.

You skip evaluation, but still face these conditions before a payout. Unless your method builds smaller profits over multiple days, it may be hard to benefit from the low entry price.

Standard terms: 14 days from the first trade, 80% profit split and $20 minimum. The first payout fixes the loss threshold at the initial balance. EAs are not allowed. Check checkout for conditions changed by add-ons.

Source: Official Instant Lite conditions ↗

Instant24: a 24-hour account, not a long-term one.

The account ends 24 hours after the first trade. If conditions are met, its design calls for a full payout after that period. It is not suited to repeated payouts from the same account.

News trading is an interesting feature, but one big win is not enough. Alongside the 3% minimum profit, the largest winning trade must stay within 15% of total profit. It suits traders who can spread profits across several trades within 24 hours.

Standard terms: 80% profit split, 2% daily loss, 3% maximum trailing loss and 1% floating loss. Only one position may be open at a time. If the conditions are not met before time expires, a profitable account may still be ineligible for a payout.

Source: Official Instant24 conditions ↗

09

Before choosing Endurance, check payouts and the key rules.

Your profit share

Standard 80%

95% requires a paid add-on. Check the standard share as well as the advertised maximum.

Holding positions

Weekend holding allowed

Allowed in both Endurance evaluation and funded accounts. EAs are also permitted, provided they do not use prohibited methods.

Payout cycle

Every 14 days as standard

A shorter cycle or an on-demand first payout requires an add-on. Minimum payout: $20.

Funded accounts: restrictions within 3 minutes of major news

Certain opening and closing trades are restricted within 3 minutes before and after high-impact news. If you buy the news-trading add-on, check exactly which trades it covers.

Funded accounts: 3% combined floating-loss limit

Alongside daily and overall loss limits, the combined floating loss on open positions must stay within 3% of the initial balance. Multiple positions are counted together.

The basic limits: 4% daily loss and 8% overall loss

The daily limit is calculated from the higher of balance or equity at 00:00 MT server time. The overall loss floor is fixed at 8% below the initial balance: $92,000 on a 100K account. It stays there after payouts.

Broker backing is different from regulation of the prop account

Broker backing is reassuring, but RZR Funded itself is not FCA-regulated. You are signing up for a prop evaluation or funded account, not an ordinary brokerage account. We have not verified any group guarantee of capital or payouts.
