Privacy policy

Contact, listing and report forms may collect the information entered, including names or contact details when provided, URLs, message content and report attachments. Submitted form content is processed and stored by Netlify, Inc. and may be handled outside Japan.

The adopted retention and deletion policy covers submitted content, attachments, notification emails, case correspondence and related copies. Its baseline is 180 days from the original receipt. An ongoing case may be extended only with a recorded reason and next review date. Deletion is targeted within 30 days after closure, or within 30 days of a deletion request. Ordinary recovery backups have a maximum of 30 days from creation; an earlier original deadline takes priority, and deleted information must not reappear after restoration. Public X research, rule ledgers and unrelated scoring evidence are outside this specific deletion scope.

This policy has been adopted, but automatic deletion, prevention of reimport after synchronization or restoration, and completion checks across every storage location have not yet been implemented and verified. Access controls, hosting logs, processing locations and conditions for external services still require confirmation. Please do not include passwords, identity documents or unnecessary personal information in a form.
