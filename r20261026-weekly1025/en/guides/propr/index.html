02

You can wait for your best setups. No need to fill trading days.

There is no evaluation deadline and no minimum trading days. Even if profits concentrate on one day, you don’t need to trade more to dilute that share.

What you trade are “Perps,” futures-style products with no expiry. Besides crypto, it covers instruments referencing stocks, indices, commodities and FX. Buying GOLD does not mean receiving physical gold.

Crypto fees: Maker 0.015% / Taker 0.045%.

No markup of Propr’s own. Fees apply to both buying and selling.

Maker is the side placing orders on the book; Taker takes orders already on the book. Even limit orders can be Taker if they fill immediately.

Example calculated at $10,000 for both opening and closing. GOLD, stocks, FX and others use the rates of the relevant HIP-3 markets. Funding while holding is also reflected in P&L.

If you’re used to LOTs, convert to instrument quantity first.

Don’t enter LOT sizes as they are; replace them with the contract quantity of the account you usually use.

What is not restricted
  • No minimum trading days or evaluation deadline.
  • No consistency rule or profit cap.
  • News trading and weekend holding OK.
  • No obligation to set an SL.
  • Scalping, swing trading and EAs / bots allowed.
  • Supports copy trading, including between your own Propr accounts.

You must stay within the daily loss and maximum loss limits. Prohibited conduct is summarized at the end.

05

How trading works, and the rules to keep in mind.

What you can check on-chain

The evaluation is a simulated account. On Funded accounts, Propr chooses A-book / B-book for each trade and shows the type on the trading screen.

  • A-book:Matching trades are sent to Hyperliquid and recorded on-chain.
  • B-book:Recorded and processed inside Propr.

You can also check the history of payout transfers. Using the public dashboard and per-trade records, you can follow the business and how trades are filled.

Prohibited conduct and account limits

Prohibited acts include cancelling out P&L with opposite trades between accounts or with external accounts, sharing or selling accounts, and exploiting delays or glitches. Re-buying evaluation accounts to repeatedly bet at maximum risk is also covered.

The combined limit for Funded accounts is 300K.

Who does it suit?

A prop firm for people who want to choose by looking at the operating numbers, and wait for their best setups without forcing more trades.

I would start by comparing the fixed-type 1STEP. Rather than deciding on the entry fee alone, I’d pick limits that can absorb my usual stop losses and losing streaks.
