01 / FX & CFDs / Smart contracts

FundedHive

Trading conditions and payouts that arrive fast. Why HIVE appeals.

The first things Ponchai liked about HIVE were the trading conditions and fast payouts. HIVE Coins earned on passing, together with PFP, can also reduce the cash needed for the next account. That is an appealing combination if you want to keep upfront spending low. But there is a tough rule after passing: a move to B-book at −1%. We cover that too.

RZR Funded

Major broker backing. A wide choice of plans.

RZR Funded combines an established operator, good trading conditions and a choice of plans. Our pick is the 3STEP at $299 for 100K. Passing takes time, but the price and post-pass terms are attractive. We also look at Express for a faster evaluation, and how the Instant options differ.

03 / On-chain / Smart contracts

Hypernova

Less waiting. More focus on trading.

Reached your profit target, but still cannot pass because you need more trading days? With Hypernova, you do not have to add trades just to fill the calendar. There are no minimum trading days or consistency rules, and funded traders can withdraw without waiting for a scheduled payout date. That could suit traders whose best setups appear only occasionally.

Propr

A closer look at the numbers behind the business.

Payout records can be visible while a firm's own revenue remains a mystery. Propr publishes those business numbers on a dashboard. Revenue, payouts and displayed reserves are there for you to look up. It is a prop firm worth exploring if transparency matters to you.
