Editorial policy & disclaimer

About this site

PROP CHECK is a site that organizes and compares information that helps you choose a prop firm. It brings together user reviews, payout and account reports, each firm’s official information and the history of plan and rule changes, and presents the “established facts” we could confirm separately from “unconfirmed information.” It provides reference information so you can see differences that a score alone does not show and consider which firm suits you.

Editorial policy

We aim to deliver accurate and objective information and edit according to the following policies.

How posts are counted and what we confirm

We count and check data based on the original text of the posts we have saved. The post counts we publish are tallies of post content; we have not actually verified or substantiated the trades, payouts received or firm decisions described in them.

For this reason, post counts cannot be treated as “the actual number of users,” “the number of people harmed” or “the number of independent incidents.” Nor can these counts be used to predict or judge “the probability of being refused” or “whether payments will be made in the future.”

About the “reference score”

The “reference score” shown in the list is a reference value for comparison, calculated by applying the same method to every firm. By default the list is sorted from highest to lowest reference score, but this order is not a ranking of safety and does not guarantee future payments.

How we update and correct information

The homepage and alerts are updated according to the following policy.

Homepage

Alerts (TELEGRAM)

We announce information such as rule changes, payout refusals and account suspensions (bans) as quickly as possible, stating where the information came from.
Anything we have not yet confirmed is clearly marked “unconfirmed.”

Data aggregation, official materials and what is displayed on the site may each have different update dates. Please check the “reference date” shown on each page or item.

When we revise content after reviewing the scope of saved materials or our judgments, we record the version and the update date and replace the content.

Disclaimer

We strive to keep information accurate and up to date, but published content may contain errors or updates may be delayed.

Published content is not investment advice, nor is it any legal, tax or financial guarantee.

Before buying a firm’s plan or using its services, always check the latest information in that firm’s official terms, such as eligible regions, fees and payout conditions, and make your own decision.
