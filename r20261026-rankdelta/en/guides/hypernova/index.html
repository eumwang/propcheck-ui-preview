What is not restricted
  • There is no consistency rule, minimum trading days, evaluation deadline or profit cap.
  • You can trade during news and hold over the weekend.
  • Setting an SL is not mandatory.
  • You can use scalping, swing, grid and your own EAs or bots on your own account.

Automated trading is allowed on your own account. Third-party copying and opposite trades between accounts are prohibited.

02

From taking profit to receiving USDC.

Even with a small profit, you can receive it if the conditions are met.There is no minimum payout or waiting period, and requests are processed by a smart contract. USDC transfer records can be checked on-chain. The officially tallied average time to arrive is 6.1 seconds.

Evaluation and funded account conditions are recorded in TradingAccounts, and the Vault checks the profit split and withdrawable amount before processing payouts. The operator-managed reserve wallet, Vault balance, and top-up and payment history can be checked on Arbitrum.

Payouts are held if sanctions or rules for the target region apply.

03

Costs and order handling for perpetual futures.

What you trade are “Perps,” futures-style products with no expiry. Besides crypto, there are instruments referencing stocks, indices and commodities. You don’t receive the underlying. Check the quantity units and trading hours before using them.

No added fee markup of its own. Also check holding costs.

Trading fees differ by instrument group and maker / taker, and are calculated per fill as “quantity × fill price × rate.” Limit orders that fill immediately are also taker. Check funding payments, spreads and slippage separately from trading fees.

Prices, order book data and funding rates use Hyperliquid data. Orders are filled by Hypernova’s own engine and are not routed to Hyperliquid.

Source: official rulebook “Tradeable markets”

05

There are some restrictions on strategies and the number of accounts.

Watch out for external signals and changing strategy after the evaluation

There are restrictions on third-party copying and signals, hedging between accounts, and major strategy changes from evaluation to funded account. For your own bots too, check conditions such as using them on a single account of your own.

Multiple accounts and loss checks

The combined limit for evaluation and funded accounts is 300K, with up to 2 evaluations running at once. The daily limit is calculated from the balance at 00:00 UTC and monitored on equity including floating P&L. Reaching a limit is treated as a failure, so leave room before the boundary.

Who does it suit?

You can wait for your best setups, and receive qualifying profits without waiting for a payout date. It suits people who value this combination. But don’t choose a plan on the entry fee alone; first check whether your usual stop losses and losing streaks fit within the loss limits.
