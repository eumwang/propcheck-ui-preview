Privacy policy

1. Basic policy

PROP CHECK (the “Site”) complies with laws on the protection of personal information and handles the information it obtains appropriately, within the scope needed for its purposes of use.

2. Information we obtain and purposes of use

For inquiries, we obtain the information you enter, such as your name, a reply email address, the content of your inquiry and related URLs, and use it to respond, to check and correct published information, and to handle consultations about rights.

For listing requests and case submissions, we obtain the firm concerned, what you report, when it happened, the firm’s response, already-public URLs, contact details and similar information, and use it to check, classify and edit what we publish, to verify facts and to prevent fraudulent submissions.

We also use publicly available posts and similar material to analyze and classify information about firms and to check the basis for published content. Where public information includes personal information, we handle it to the extent necessary.

3. Publication and management of information

We do not publish contact details or the materials we receive themselves. After review, we may publish a summary of a submitted case written so that individuals cannot be identified, or already-public source URLs.

We take necessary and appropriate security measures to prevent leakage, loss or damage. We review whether retention is necessary and strive to delete information we no longer need without delay, except where the law requires retention.

Our retention and deletion policy covers the message body, attachments, received emails, handling records and copies of these received through forms. The basic retention limit is 180 days from first receipt. We may extend this where needed to continue handling a case. After handling ends, we aim to delete within 30 days.

Regular restore backups are kept for up to 30 days from creation; if the deletion deadline for the original information is earlier, that deadline takes priority. Our policy is not to bring back deleted information when restoring. Materials collected from public X posts, records of rule changes and unrelated scoring evidence as a whole are not subject to bulk deletion under this policy.

Logs and similar data whose retention the service provider controls are handled separately from the Site’s retention policy.

4. External services and provision to third parties

When we provide personal data to a third party, we obtain your consent in advance, except where the law provides otherwise. When we entrust handling to a contractor to the extent needed for our purposes, we supervise the contractor appropriately.

What you enter in forms is sent to and stored by the form feature of Netlify, Inc. It may be processed outside Japan.

We use the TypeSafe AI API to classify public X posts. We may send the text of the posts being classified, the context of reply-to and quoted posts we retrieved, expanded URLs, the firm or service concerned, and attribute information needed for classification.

According to TypeSafe’s public description, the service is hosted in the United States and inputs are not used to train or fine-tune models. However, the retention period is described as the period needed to provide the service, and the Site does not guarantee a fixed number of days or immediate deletion.

The Site does not set or guarantee the processing region, retention period or deletion completion date for form content, attachments and backups on Netlify.

5. Cookies and browser storage

The Site uses your browser’s storage to keep display settings. You can delete stored information from your browser settings.

We also use Google Analytics (GA4), provided by Google LLC, on all pages to understand how the Site is accessed. GA4 uses cookies, and information such as the pages you view and your browsing environment is sent to Google. Google’s privacy policy applies to how the sent information is handled.

Logs are handled for delivery monitoring and security. The types of logs and their retention conditions depend on the services and settings used, and this does not mean they are deleted on the same 180-day / 30-day schedule as the form retention policy.

Some English and Korean guide pages use Google’s image delivery service to display firm logos. When the images load, your browser communicates with Google and its image delivery hosts (under gstatic.com). The image URLs include information about the firm website being displayed.

For these images, we specify a setting that does not send the Site’s referrer information.

In its published privacy policy, Google explains how it collects IP addresses and other information in connection with communications with its services. Even though the Site suppresses the referrer, this does not mean that communication for external images is anonymous. The provider’s policy applies to how information is handled at the image delivery host.

You can restrict or delete cookies in your browser settings. However, restricting cookies is not the same as stopping external images from loading. Depending on the restrictions, some displays or features may not be available.

The Site does not set or guarantee the retention period or deletion timing of access records or security logs.

6. Referral links

The Site contains referral links through which the operator receives a referral commission when you sign up. For information collection and use of cookies at the linked destination, please check each provider’s privacy policy.

7. Disclosure requests and inquiries

For requests to notify the purpose of use of, disclose, correct or stop using retained personal data, or for consultations about how personal information is handled, please contact us through the inquiry form. We will verify your identity as necessary and respond in accordance with the law.

Please do not attach personal information or identity documents that are not needed for the inquiry to your first message.

Operated and edited by PONCHAI. Consultations about personal information are accepted through the existing contact form.

We aim to respond to deletion and similar requests within 30 days of receipt. Such requests are handled by the operator (PONCHAI). For identity verification, we generally use the existing reply channel or submission receipt and ask only for the minimum information needed.

For deletion and similar requests, we act on the storage locations the Site can check. We do not guarantee that deletion from every storage location, including backups, has been completed.

8. Revisions

We review this policy in response to changes in how we handle information or in the law, and announce the changes and their effective date on this page. Where required by law, we give separate notice or obtain consent.

Cookie policy

2. When you follow external links such as official websites or referral destinations, each provider’s cookie policy applies.

3. If we introduce advertising or change the analytics we use in the future, we will confirm the purpose, provider, retention period and how you can opt out, and update this page.
