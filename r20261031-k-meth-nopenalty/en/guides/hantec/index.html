04

Endurance 3STEP | The top pick, even if it takes time.

3STEP’s strengths are not just its price.Each phase has a 6% profit target and a fixed 8% maximum loss. There is no consistency rule, and the minimum of 3 trading days does not require winning on all 3 days. You don’t have to pile on extra trades to create profitable days.

Before buying a big account, keep budget for another attempt.

What gives me pause is the time it takes to pass. Even after hitting 6% once, the next account starts from scratch. You go through that three times, so failing partway also costs time.

A 100K account for $299 is tempting, but I want to avoid spending my whole budget on the first attempt. Deciding how many attempts you can afford and choosing the account size within that is easier to keep up, I think.

See detailed conditions and diagrams

After passing, there is still no consistency rule, and you can hold over the weekend and use EAs. Since it’s easy to keep using your best strategy, I feel the three evaluations are worth taking on. On funded accounts, a 3% floating-loss limit and restrictions around news apply, soalso check the payout and trading conditions later in this article.

Source: trading days · targets · period

05

Express 1STEP | Another recommendation, aiming for the first payout.

If you want to finish the evaluation fast, choose Express.There is no minimum number of trading days, so if you meet the conditions you can pass in as little as one day. In the extreme, even with one trade. If you hit the 10% target on a good opportunity, you don’t have to keep trading just to fill days.

After the first payout, your room for losses becomes small.

The first payout locks the loss line at the initial balance. If you withdraw all profits and bring the balance back to the initial amount, you have no room left for losses.To keep the account going you need to leave profits in it, so it suits using the first payout as a milestone.

For example, with 100K, if both the post-payout balance and the loss line are $100,000, you have zero room for the next loss. If you plan to keep withdrawing repeatedly, this condition matters a lot.

Even if you pass at the earliest, payouts require a standard 14-day wait. An on-demand first payout is a paid add-on. On funded accounts, you also need to keep to the 3% floating-loss limit and the restrictions around news.

07

Instant | No evaluation. Choose from 3 types by how you’ll use it.

For those who want to skip the evaluation and start, there is Instant. But the 3 types are used quite differently. Instant24 in particular is an account that ends after 24 hours, so it doesn’t suit people who want to trade long term.

TypeSuited toKey points before buying
Instant FundingSkip the evaluation and trade with no consistency ruleBasic display: daily 6% · total loss 6% · floating loss 3%. Payout conditions are separate
Instant LiteKeep the entry fee low and spread profits over several days20% consistency · 5 profit days · a 3% buffer you cannot withdraw
Instant24Stack several small profits within a 24-hour windowEnds after 24 hours. Minimum profit 3% · 15% per-trade consistency

Instant Funding | For those who want to skip the evaluation and start trading.

Instant Funding skips the evaluation and has no consistency rule. If your profits tend to concentrate on particular days, this is the type to look at before Lite or Instant24.

Watch out for the 3% floating-loss limit. Even if daily 6% and total loss 6% look roomy, you may hit it first while holding positions. The standard payout frequency is 14 days. Weekend holding and news trading conditions change with the add-ons you choose.

Source: official Instant Funding display

Instant Lite | For those who want to build up profits with a low entry fee.

Instant Lite lets you start without an evaluation and keeps the entry fee low. But it requires spreading profits over several days, so it’s hard to use if you want to withdraw from a few big wins.

Each payout cycle requires 5 profitable days of at least 0.5%. On top of that, the biggest profit day must be no more than 20% of total profit. The first 3% cannot be withdrawn and stays in the account as a buffer. There are also limits of daily 3%, trailing maximum 5% and floating loss 1%.

You can skip the evaluation, but there are all these conditions before a payout. Unless your strategy stacks small profits over several days, it’s hard to take advantage of the low entry fee.

Standard is 14 days after the first trade, an 80% profit split and a $20 minimum. The first payout locks the loss line at the initial balance. No EAs. Check conditions that change with add-ons on the purchase screen.

Source: Instant Lite official conditions

Instant24 | An account that ends within 24 hours, separate from ongoing trading.

The account ends 24 hours after your first trade, and is designed for a full payout after the period ends if you meet the conditions. It doesn’t suit repeated payouts from the same account.

Being able to trade the news is interesting, but you can’t just win big once and be done. Besides a 3% minimum profit, your single most profitable trade must be no more than 15% of total profit. For those who can spread profits across several trades within 24 hours.

Standard is an 80% profit split, daily loss 2%, trailing maximum loss 3% and floating loss 1%. One position at a time. If you don’t meet the conditions before time runs out, you may not be able to withdraw even with a profit.

Source: Instant24 official conditions

09

Before continuing with Endurance, check payouts and key conditions.

Profit share

Standard 80%

95% is a paid add-on. Judge the maximum and the standard conditions separately.

Holding style

Weekend holding allowed

Endurance allows it on both evaluation and funded accounts. EAs are also allowed as long as they don’t use prohibited strategies.

Payout cycle

Standard 14 days

Shorter cycles and an on-demand first payout are add-ons. The minimum payout is $20.

Funded account: restrictions 3 minutes before and after major news

For 3 minutes before and after high-impact news, there are restrictions on eligible new entries and closes. If you choose the news-trading add-on, check its scope in the purchase conditions.

Funded account: 3% floating loss across all open positions

On top of the daily and maximum loss limits, the combined floating loss of open positions must stay within 3% of the initial balance. Multiple positions are counted together.

Daily 4% · maximum 8%: check them as the basic conditions

The daily limit is calculated from the higher of the balance and equity at 00:00 MT server time. The maximum loss is fixed at −8% of the initial balance. For 100K, the reference is $92,000, and it doesn’t change after payouts.

Keep the broker’s support separate from how the account itself is regulated

The broker’s support is reassuring, but RZR Funded itself is not regulated by the FCA. What you sign up for is a prop evaluation/funded account, not an ordinary brokerage account. We have also not been able to confirm any group guarantee of principal or payouts.
