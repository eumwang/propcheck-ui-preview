Compensation for unexpected slippage on your SL.

05

Payout conditions | To receive your A-book profits.

If your A-book P&L is positive, payouts are instant from a single trade.

The basic requirement is thatyour A-book P&L is positive and you have withdrawable profit. Funded accounts have no minimum number of trading days for payouts; you can withdraw after a single trade if the conditions are met.

If past A-book losses or the PFP funded-account fee remain, profits go to settling those first. For Classic / PFP, you also need to meet conditions such as a $50 minimum payout after the split and fee settlement.

Sources: A-book P&L and fee settlement; no minimum trading days on funded accounts

Unfavorable conditions to check before buying

At −1% you move to B-book. Profits made while recovering cannot be withdrawn.

HIVE’s big weakness is the AADS rule: when a funded account falls about 1% below its initial balance, open positions are closed and the account moves to B-book.If you only look at the usual loss limits, it is easy to miss this payout condition. Profits made on B-book cannot be withdrawn as cash.

Even if you stay in the funded account, trading profits on B-book are not eligible for cash payouts. You first need to recover to the initial balance and return to A-book, then offset earlier A-book losses with new A-book profits.Even after your balance recovers, payouts require offsetting A-book losses.

What happens on a 100K account?

A simplified example to explain the mechanism. Actual switch and settlement amounts, fees and account status will change the figures. This restriction is separate from the failure line.

It is especially harsh for strategies that tolerate large temporary drawdowns, or for people who want to make money from recovering out of a loss too.Don’t choose on the low price alone; first decide whether this restriction fits your trading.

Source: official AADS explanation

Who can make the most of the trading environment and payout speed?

The deciding point is whether your usual strategy can handle the −1% switch. If your strategy keeps risk small enough to stay on A-book, you can receive qualifying profits without waiting for the next payout date.

Coins for passing and PFP’s pay-from-profits also reduce the cash you need up front.But profits earned on B-book never become eligible for payout.Along with the trading environment and costs, consider whether the −1% switch fits your strategy.

With multiple accounts, watch out for trading or copying the same instrument

There are restrictions on copy trading across several funded accounts and on holding duplicate positions in the same instrument. Even with different currency pairs, correlated price moves can stack losses. If you add accounts, manage the total across all accounts as well as each account’s risk.

Payouts also have a minimum and a daily limit

For Classic / PFP, the minimum payout is $50 after the split and fee settlement. Under the terms, the daily limit is $1,000 per ERC20 address. If you use several accounts, note that the limit is per address. Eligible A-book profit and fee payment conditions take priority.

08

Instant Growth | Level up and grow the account.

Turn each level’s achievement into the next account.

Instant Growth is a plan where you grow the account little by little. You start at level 1 and aim for 6% profit at each level. The next level’s fee is paid from profits, so it suits people who want to grow the account rather than take profits right away.

There is no daily loss limit, but there are limits of 6% maximum loss and 2% per trade.

The first level is an evaluation on B-book. Finishing level 1 and withdrawing instead of moving on is allowed once per user. From level 2, you proceed on A-book. You cannot freely withdraw funded-account profits right after purchase.

Three points about how it progresses

  • Lv1 is an evaluation on B-book. Stopping at Lv1 and withdrawing is allowed once per user.
  • From Lv2, AADS applies. If you move to B-book at −1%, profits while recovering cannot be used for cash payouts.
  • Instant is 10% off with PONCHAI. The start fee is normally $299, or $269.10 with the code. Coins cannot be used.
