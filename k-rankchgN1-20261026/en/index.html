AI CLASSIFICATION

Collected posts and AI classification

Counts separate collected, AI-classified, and firm-related posts, and verified first-hand payout/account report groups.

Post counts are firm–post pairs; first-hand report counts are firm–report-group pairs (duplicates merged, so one group may cover several posts). They are not users, victims, refusal rates, or independent incidents, and do not confirm the claims.

How to read the numbers

Start with the overall reference score, then check the review breakdown and the firsthand report results separately.

1

Overall reference score

Combines payout eligibility 50%, firsthand reviews 20% and operational foundation 30%, minus any history-change deduction where applicable. It is a reference value.

It is not an actual payout-success rate or safety probability. Each item uses its own aggregation period.
2

Review breakdown

Switching the display period changes this breakdown.
3

Firsthand report results

We count firsthand reports of payout and account problems that we could confirm on X.

For what is counted, the scoring criteria and caveats, see “Methodology”.
