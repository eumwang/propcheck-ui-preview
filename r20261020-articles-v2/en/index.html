GUIDE

How to read the numbers

Start with the overall reference score, then check review breakdown and first-hand report results separately.

1

Overall reference score

A reference indicator combining payout and account reports 50%, X firsthand experience 20%, and operational foundation 30%. It is not an actual payout-success rate or safety probability.

2

Review breakdown

Posts accepted in original-text review are grouped as positive, mixed, or negative for the selected display period. Overall scores use the approved 730-day basis.

3

First-hand reports

Only ordinary first-hand reports are counted here, separating payout reports from issue reports.
